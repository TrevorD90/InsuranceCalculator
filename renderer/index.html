About the numbers

      

        Cost sharing is calculated on the plan's allowed amount, never the billed charge.
        Each plan carries its own two assumptions for turning one into the other — open a plan
        card to change them.
      

      

        Balance billing and non-covered services are shown separately and in a warning colour
        because, unlike everything else, nothing caps them — they never count toward the
        deductible or the out-of-pocket maximum.
